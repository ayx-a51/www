We design and build mobile apps end to end. Whether one is any good comes down to whether it does its job and feels right in the hand — not to the paperwork around it.

The work

  • vivevo

    Android · Free

    A one-tap journal for the five areas that shape a day — mood, energy, productivity, diet and relationship.

    See more

    Five seconds of honesty, once a day. You pick the picture that matches, and the month fills in behind you — so a bad stretch shows up as a shape rather than a memory. Streaks freeze on their own when you miss a day.

    vivevo.net Google Play

  • QIZU

    Android · Free

    Flashcards you type. Draw a card, type the answer, and the app checks it on the spot.

    See more

    Typing is what makes it stick — “close enough” never sneaks past. Weak cards come back on their own, with no schedules and no due-date pile-ups. HSK, JLPT, TOPIK and CEFR word lists are built in.

    qizu.net Google Play

  • FOCUSQUARE

    Android · Free

    A focus timer built around one tap. Eight customisable presets, widgets and cross-device sync.

    See more

    Tap a square and it is running — fullscreen colour, no text, nothing to read. The countdown lives in the notification shade and keeps going even if the app is killed. Statistics run from today to all-time.

    A51.io/FOCUSQUARE Google Play

  • TrackAllerg

    Android

    Allergy and symptom tracking, matched against the pollen and weather you were actually in.

    See more

    Symptoms, medicines and notes in four taps a day. Local pollen counts and weather are pulled in automatically, and the overlay shows which triggers actually track your symptoms — and which ones you only assumed did.

How we work

  • One team, the whole product

    Design, code, store listing, support. Nothing is handed to a subcontractor, so nothing falls between two of them.

  • Usability is the spec

    If a screen needs explaining, it isn’t finished. We would rather cut a feature than bury the one that matters.

  • Light on process

    No account managers, no change-request forms. You talk to the people building the thing.
